Look beyond the thermostat.

Comfort is the result of temperature, airflow and how a space is used. Describe the change you notice; you do not need to diagnose the cause before getting in touch.

Rooms that feel different from one another, weak airflow or changes in operating sound are useful observations to include in a request. Let us know whether the issue is constant or appears at a particular time of day.

For commercial properties, equipment access and occupied hours help shape the service conversation. For a home, include the type of system and the areas affected. Frimax confirms availability and the next step with you directly.
